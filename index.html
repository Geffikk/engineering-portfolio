Football forecasting framework

Predicts football events from engineered features, then checks whether those predictions would actually have made money against real bookmaker odds.

How I work

What running my own systems taught me

Nobody hands me a runbook, an on-call rota or someone else's post-mortem. These four habits came out of incidents I had to diagnose and fix myself.

Measure before optimising

A latency waterfall killed one planned optimisation and a profile produced a real one: 2.70s down to 0.19s.

Absence is a first-class alert

My worst incidents were silences, not crashes. Nine days of dead production with every alert green.

Refusing beats guessing

Stale prices, unreachable venues and data too poor to value all stop the work with a named reason.

Every number is a measurement

Money comes from measured fills, never the plan. Valuations hash their inputs so they can be replayed.
